## How to use these resources

Every performance claim needs a workload, a configuration and a measurement.

Read the conditions beside each finding. Hypothetical calculations are not measured product performance, a universal design limit or evidence of product certification. The three design images in the buyer materials are concepts, with their rights and image-treatment notes retained in the documents.
